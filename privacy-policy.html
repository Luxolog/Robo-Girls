ROBO GIRLS Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

Before launch: Confirm the legal entity operating ROBO GIRLS, its Information Officer/privacy contact, email marketing platform, retention period and any cross-border processing. This is an implementation draft, not legal certification.

Responsible party

ROBO GIRLS is the intended responsible party for the newsletter. The legal entity name and verified privacy contact must be confirmed before live collection. Privacy enquiries may initially be directed to info@robogirls.co.za.

Information collected

The newsletter form collects your email address and records your separate privacy notice acknowledgement and affirmative marketing opt-in.

Purpose and choice

Your email address is used to send new styles, collection news and brand updates. The marketing checkbox is a separate, affirmative choice and must remain unticked by default. You can unsubscribe at any time. Do not subscribe someone else without their permission.

Providers, security and retention

Before the newsletter is connected, identify the email marketing provider, document its operator agreement, security controls and any cross-border transfers. Restrict access to authorised staff and retain subscriber information only while the subscription remains active or as required by law. Delete or suppress records appropriately after unsubscribe.

Your POPIA rights

Subject to POPIA, you may request access to or correction of your information, object to processing, withdraw consent and request deletion where applicable. Contact info@robogirls.co.za. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
